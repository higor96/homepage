Marcos M. Raimundo is an Assistant Professor at University of Campinas (UNICAMP), in the Institute of Computing (IC). He is also a faculty member of the REasoning for COmplex Data laboratory (Recod.ai). His research interests include Machine Learning, Multi-objective Optimization, Ethical AI, Mathematical Programming.

Multi-Objective Dynamics of Learning

2026 – ongoing
Multi-Objective Optimization
Deep Learning
Representation Learning
Learning Dynamics
Pareto Sensitivity
Optimization Algorithms

A research line that studies learning as an inherently multi-objective process, seeking to understand how objectives compete and cooperate while a representation is being formed, and how to steer training so that the learned space improves all objectives rather than trading one for another.

Mechanistic Interpretability and Latent Controllability in Large Language Models

2026 – ongoing
Mechanistic Interpretability
Representation Engineering
Large Language Models
Activation Steering
Latent Geometry

Research on mechanistic interpretability, representation engineering (RepE), and attention steering in foundation models — investigating internal activation geometry to detect hallucinations, prevent scope drift, mitigate demographic bias, and enforce safe behavioral alignment without full-model retraining.

2026 – ongoing
Responsible AI

A two-stage sequential decision-making framework combining offline reinforcement learning, contextual bandits, and multi-objective optimization to eliminate harmful feedback loops, mitigate selective labels (reject inference), and ensure long-term fairness and policy flexibility.
